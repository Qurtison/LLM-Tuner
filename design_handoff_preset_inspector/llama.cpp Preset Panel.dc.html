1Three structural takes — llama.cpp preset inspector

Same content model in all three: every flag from the dump lives in one of twelve task groups, deprecated and removed flags sit in a separate Archive, and flag names surface on hover rather than as labels. What differs is the spine — how you get from 380px of screen to 214 settings. No CLI preview anywhere, per your call.
